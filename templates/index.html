How it works

From field notebook to footprint
in four steps.

The calculator follows a cotton season the way it happens on the farm, so the data your team already records is the data you enter.

01

Register the farm

Location down to tehsil, cotton area, soil texture, drainage and climate.

02

Log the season

Seed, fertiliser, irrigation, sprays, field operations, residue and picking.

03

See the hotspots

Total CO₂e, intensity per kg of seed cotton and lint, and where it comes from.

04

Share the report

A print-ready PDF with inputs, factors and results for buyers and auditors.

SAWiE runs a distributed network for agricultural monitoring in Pakistan, combining geospatial data with machine learning and IoT.

Its farm advisory gives farmers real-time, reliable and unbiased information, helping smallholders and large farms deal with climate challenges and build resilient food systems.

CarbonLens brings that approach to carbon. A field officer or farmer enters one cotton season of inputs and gets a farm-gate footprint with the breakdown behind it. The method follows the Cool Farm Tool structure and IPCC guidance, with factors adapted for Pakistan: local fertiliser products, tube-well energy sources, common tractor sizes and the district and tehsil lists used in the field.

What SAWiE does

Advisory across the farming cycle

Soil analysis & mapping

Satellite and open datasets to assess soil moisture and structure for farm management.

Crop planning & sowing

Best sowing dates from weather history and forecasts, with crop and variety recommendations.

Fertiliser & irrigation

Fertiliser recommendations and irrigation schedules that cut water loss and cost.

Harvest & yield forecasting

Harvest timing that avoids bad weather, plus early yield estimates for supply planning.

Pest & disease alerts

Early warnings so farmers can act before an outbreak spreads.

Farm productivity

All of the above combined, so decisions across the season rest on data.
